Research Interests

My work sits at the intersection of abstract theory and concrete implementation. I am currently focused on:

  • Quantum Computing Algorithms

    Developing error-correction codes for noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) devices.

  • Computational Astrophysics

    Simulating galaxy formation using parallelized N-body simulations.

  • AI for Scientific Discovery

    Applying deep learning models to identify patterns in high-energy particle collision data.
